# What's New in TheoShift v4.2.0

**Released:** February 9, 2026

---

## 🎯 Overview

We've made TheoShift easier to use with three major improvements:

1. **Find Events Faster** - Search and organize your events with smart grouping
2. **Save Your Locations** - Reuse common event locations and see them on maps
3. **Quick Volunteer Info** - View volunteer details without leaving your page

---

## ✨ What's New

### 🔍 Find Events Faster

**The Problem:**
Scrolling through long lists of events to find what you need was time-consuming, especially with many completed or archived events.

**What We Fixed:**
Your event selection page is now organized and searchable!

**What You Can Do:**

**Search for Events**
- Type in the search bar to instantly filter events by name, description, or location
- See how many events match your search
- Click the X to clear your search

**See Event Relationships**
- Events with sub-events (like a convention with multiple sessions) now show their relationship
- Click the arrow next to a parent event to see all its child events
- Child events are highlighted in purple so they're easy to spot

**Organized by Status**
- Events are grouped into sections: Current, Upcoming, Completed, Archived, and Cancelled
- Current events appear at the top so you can get to work immediately
- Completed and archived events are hidden by default to keep your view clean
- Click any section header to show or hide that group

**How to Use It:**
1. Go to the event selection page
2. Use the search bar to find specific events
3. Click section headers to expand or collapse groups
4. Click the arrow next to events to see their sub-events

---

### 📍 Save Your Locations

**The Problem:**
Typing the same venue addresses over and over for recurring events was tedious and error-prone.

**What We Fixed:**
You can now save locations and reuse them across multiple events!

**What You Can Do:**

**Save Locations**
- Create a library of your frequently used venues
- Add addresses using Google Maps autocomplete
- See locations on an interactive map
- Add notes about parking, room numbers, or other details

**Use Saved Locations**
- When creating or editing an event, select from your saved locations
- See popular locations at the top of the list
- Still type a custom address if needed

**Manage Your Library**
- View all your locations at Admin → Location Library
- Search and filter your saved locations
- See which locations are used most often
- Edit or deactivate locations you no longer need

**How to Use It:**
1. **To save a location:** Go to Admin → Location Library → Create Location
2. **To use a saved location:** When creating an event, click the location dropdown and select from your saved locations
3. **To manage locations:** Go to Admin → Location Library to view, edit, or organize your locations

---

### 👥 Quick Volunteer Info

**The Problem:**
Checking volunteer details meant navigating to a different page, losing your place in your current work.

**What We Fixed:**
Hover over or click any volunteer name to see their information instantly!

**What You Can Do:**

**Hover for Quick Preview**
- Move your mouse over any volunteer name
- After a brief moment, a popup appears with their details
- Move your mouse away and the popup disappears

**Click for Full Details**
- Click a volunteer name to lock the popup open
- View their full contact information, assignments, and status
- Click "Edit Volunteer" to make changes
- Click the X or anywhere outside to close

**What You'll See:**
- Contact information (email and phone)
- Current status (Active, Verified, etc.)
- What they're assigned to
- Who oversees them
- Their availability

**How to Use It:**
1. On any page showing volunteer names, hover over a name for a quick preview
2. Click a name to keep the popup open
3. Click "Edit Volunteer" if you need to make changes
4. Click outside the popup or the X to close it
