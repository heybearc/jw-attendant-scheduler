## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Early Check-In works better on phones
Mobile Check-In is easier to use across Safari, Chrome, and Firefox.

**What changed:**
- Larger tap targets and safer spacing around the home indicator
- Search no longer zooms the page on iPhone
- Staff can open Early Check-In when they have rights (route was blocked before)
