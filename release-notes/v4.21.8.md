# What's New in Version 4.21.8

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Volunteer email broadcast — clearer who receives “all active”

When you choose to email **all active volunteers**, the list now lines up with **active** people on the volunteer roster (including having an email on file). That matches what you expect from the Active view on the Volunteers page.

### More reliable “From” address on emails

If your server did not have a dedicated **From** address configured, some messages could fail or look inconsistent. TheoShift now falls back in a sensible way so routine notices are more likely to deliver.

### Better feedback when emailing many people

When you send to a large group, you get clearer messaging about progress and results—including when something could not be delivered to a specific address—so you are not left wondering if the send finished.

### Chat rollout emails use the same volunteer rules

Optional emails when event chat is rolled out now follow the same active-roster logic for consistency.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No change to how you open **Email volunteers** or **Email selected**—only who is included for “all active” and how clearly you see send results.
