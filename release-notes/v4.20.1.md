# What's New in Version 4.20.1

**Released:** May 5, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Easier chat navigation

The event chat sidebar is organized more like common team-chat apps. You will see a **New message** action to start a conversation or pick a **position channel** from a search list, so you are not scrolling through a long list of stations.

**Volunteer chat** uses the same idea: browse position channels when you need them, and use **New message** for direct messages.

### Staff and volunteer accounts

If your login is linked to your volunteer profile by an administrator, that link is now honored for **direct messages** in event chat when you are on the event volunteer list. You do not need a separate step on the volunteer page just for chat.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No change to how you open chat from the event or volunteer dashboard.
- If something still looks off, ask your administrator to confirm your user is linked to the correct volunteer and that you are on the event’s volunteer list.

---

## 📞 Need Help?

- Open **Help** from the app and read **Event Chat**.
- Contact your system administrator.
- Use **Send Feedback** if something is not working as expected.
