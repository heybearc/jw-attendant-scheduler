# What's New in Version 4.30.0

**Released:** July 31, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Request an IVS volunteer from your dashboard
If you are on the IVS list for an event, you can submit a new volunteer request from the volunteer dashboard (**Request** tab).

**What you can do:**
- Enter name, congregation, email, and phone (required)
- Optionally add department, notes, and early-entry days
- See your past submissions and their approval status

Requests start as **Pending** for staff on IVS Approvals. They are not approved automatically.

### Email and phone on IVS Approvals
Staff can see and edit email and phone on the IVS Approvals list, when adding or editing someone, and in the IVS export.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Only people already on the event’s **IVS list** can submit requests (not the broader Early Check-In or positions roster alone)
- Staff review and approve as usual on **IVS Approvals**
- Help Center pages for IVS Approvals and mobile features explain the new flow
