# What's New in Version 4.29.6

**Released:** July 27, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Roster team can use Early Check-In
People on the event Volunteers roster were seeing Check-In greyed out or Access Denied unless they also had a position assignment.

**What changed:**
- If you are on the event roster and IVS is enabled, Check-In works
- You do not need a position assignment just to open the check-in list

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Select your event first so Check-In knows which roster to use
- Hard-refresh the volunteer app if the bottom bar still looks old
