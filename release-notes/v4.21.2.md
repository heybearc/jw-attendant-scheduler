# What's New in Version 4.21.2

**Released:** May 7, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### IVS Module on phones and tablets

The **IVS Module** (Approvals and Early Check-In) is easier to use on a small screen. Approvals shows a clear card layout instead of a wide table you have to scroll sideways. Early Check-In uses full-width buttons and stacked rows so names and actions are easy to tap at the check-in table.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required. Open the IVS Module from your event as usual.
- On a computer, Approvals still appears as the full table you are used to.
