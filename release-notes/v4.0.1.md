## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Positions Page Scroll Position

**What was fixed:**
When you refreshed the positions page, it would always jump back to the top of the page. Now it remembers where you were scrolling and takes you back to that spot.

**What you'll notice:**
- Refresh the positions page and stay at your current scroll position
- Switch between List and Grid views without losing your place
- Your view preference (List or Grid) is now remembered between visits
