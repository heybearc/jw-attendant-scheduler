# What's New in Version 4.29.14

**Released:** July 31, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Early check-in list scrolls on tablet and mobile
On tablet and some mobile layouts, the Early Check-In area could sit behind or below the visible screen so the Pending and Checked In lists never appeared when you scrolled.

The check-in page now grows with its content so you can scroll to the full list and use the Check In buttons.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Open **Check-In** from the volunteer dashboard or bottom nav and scroll normally to see everyone pending or already checked in
