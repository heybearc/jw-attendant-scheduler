# What's New in Version 3.4.0

**Released:** January 25, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### 📧 Assignment Notifications (Phase 4C - Part 1)
Volunteers now receive professional email notifications when assigned to positions, making coordination easier and reducing confusion.

**What you can do:**
- Receive automatic emails when assigned to a position
- Get notified when your assignment changes
- Receive reminders before your shift
- See all assignment details in a beautifully formatted email

**How it works:**
1. When a coordinator assigns you to a position, you'll receive an email
2. The email includes event details, position information, and shift times
3. You can click a link to view full assignment details in the app

---

### ✅ Assignment Confirmation System (Phase 4C - Part 2)
Volunteers can now confirm or decline assignments directly from email, helping coordinators plan more effectively.

**What you can do:**
- Confirm assignments with one click from your email
- Decline if you can't serve (with optional reason)
- Mark yourself as tentative if unsure
- No login required - secure token-based confirmation

**How to use it:**
1. Open your assignment notification email
2. Click "Confirm", "Tentative", or "Decline"
3. Your response is recorded instantly
4. Coordinators see your confirmation status in real-time

---

### 📅 Availability Requests (Phase 4C - Part 3)
Coordinators can now ask volunteers about availability BEFORE making assignments, reducing last-minute cancellations.

**What you can do:**
- Receive availability requests for upcoming events
- Respond with: Available, Not Available, or Partial Availability
- Specify which dates/times you're available
- View all your availability requests in one place

**How to use it:**
1. Check your email for availability requests
2. Click the response link or visit your dashboard
3. Select your availability status
4. Add notes if you have partial availability

**Benefits:**
- Coordinators know who's available before assigning
- Fewer last-minute cancellations
- Better assignment planning
- Less back-and-forth communication

## 💡 What You Need to Know

**For Volunteers:**
- Check your email for assignment notifications and availability requests

**No action required** - The system will start sending notifications automatically for new assignments.
