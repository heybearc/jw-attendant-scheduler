## ✨ Magic Link Authentication - Passwordless Login for Volunteers

We've completely redesigned how volunteers sign in to make it easier and more secure!

### 📧 What's New

**Magic Link Sign-In** - No more PINs or passwords to remember!

Volunteers now receive a secure email link to sign in. Just click the link in your email and you're automatically logged in!

**What you can do:**
- Sign in from any device without remembering a PIN
- More secure - each link is unique and expires after 24 hours
- Works great on mobile phones and tablets
- No more forgotten PINs or password resets

**How to use it:**
1. Go to the TheoShift login page
2. Click the **"Volunteer"** button
3. Enter your email address
4. Click **"Send Sign-In Link"**
5. Check your email and click the secure link
6. You're in!
