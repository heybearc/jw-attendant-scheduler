# What's New in Version 4.23.0

**Released:** June 3, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Per-day IVS early entry and check-in

IVS early entry now works by **convention day** (Friday, Saturday, Sunday) instead of a single yes/no flag.

**What you can do:**
- Mark a volunteer eligible for one day, two days, or all three
- Check volunteers in separately for each day they are eligible
- Add or remove days on the fly (for example, a Friday-only volunteer told to come Saturday)
- View who is eligible and checked in using **Today** plus **Fri / Sat / Sun** tabs
- Export reports with per-day status columns

**How to use it:**
1. Open **IVS → Approvals** and use the **Fri / Sat / Sun** toggles on each volunteer (or bulk **Set early entry days**).
2. On **IVS → Early Check-In**, pick **Today** or a specific day tab, then check in only for that day.
3. IVS volunteers on their phone use the same day tabs in **Early Check-In**.

**Removing a day:** If you turn off eligibility for a day that already has a check-in, TheoShift asks for confirmation and clears that day’s check-in record.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Volunteers eligible for multiple days are **not** auto-checked-in for every day — staff check in each day separately.
- Existing “early entry eligible” volunteers were migrated to all three days; you can narrow days anytime.
- Check-in times use the event timezone (Eastern Time by default).
