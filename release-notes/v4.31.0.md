# What's New in Version 4.31.0

**Released:** August 2, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Filter volunteers by availability
On the event **Volunteers** page, use the new availability filter to show people who are available, not available, partial, pending, or who have not been asked yet. This makes it easier to follow up before assigning positions.

### Edit shift times while assigning
When you assign someone to a position, you can open **Edit times** on the selected shift and adjust start/end (or all-day) without leaving the assign window. Times are still shared for everyone on that shift — if people are already assigned, you will be asked to confirm before saving.

---

## 🔒 Safer bulk emails

### Positions “Send Notifications” is paced and cancelable
Sending assignment emails from Positions now uses the same safe pattern as other volunteer emails: you see how many people will be emailed, sending is paced for Gmail limits, and you can **Abort** if you need to stop.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Chat push message on desktop
When push alerts are not supported, the banner no longer always talks about iPhone. Desktop browsers get a clearer message; iPhone/iPad still get Home Screen guidance.

### Positions and Volunteers on phones
Toolbar filters, status pills, and shift Edit controls are easier to tap on smaller screens.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Availability filter is on the Volunteers page toolbar
- Assignment notification sends may take longer for large events — that is intentional pacing
- Changing shift times updates the schedule for everyone on that shift
