# What's New in Version 4.21.5

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Email volunteers from an event

On the **Volunteers** tab for an event, you can send an email to:

- **Everyone you have checked**, or  
- **All active volunteers** who have an email address on file  

You choose a **subject** and write your **message**. TheoShift sends it using the same email setup your administrator configured for other notifications.

---

## 🐛 Fixes

### Selecting several volunteers

Choosing more than one volunteer with the checkboxes now works the way you expect—your selection builds up instead of replacing itself.

### IVS Approvals: Shift+click to select a range

On **IVS Approvals**, holding **Shift** and clicking another checkbox now reliably selects everyone in between on the current page.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Volunteers **without** an email in their profile are skipped.
- Your oversight role must allow managing that event (same idea as other staff tools).
