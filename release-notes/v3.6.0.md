# What's New in Version 3.6.0

**Released:** January 30, 2026

## ✨ New Features

### 🔍 Event Oversight Dashboard

A powerful new tool to help you manage and visualize oversight coverage for your events!

**What you can do:**
- See at-a-glance coverage statistics for each event
- View all overseers, assistant overseers, and keymen assignments in one place
- Identify positions that still need oversight assigned (coverage gaps)
- Track coverage percentage with color-coded indicators
- Filter positions by oversight role on the positions page

**How to use it:**
1. Go to your event dashboard
2. Click the **"🔍 Oversight Dashboard"** button in Quick Actions
3. Review coverage statistics and assignments
4. Check the coverage gaps section to see which positions need oversight

**Where to find it:**
- Quick Actions card on event dashboard
- Direct link in event navigation menu
- Help documentation in Help Center

---

### 📊 Export Reports (PDF & Excel)

Export your oversight data for offline use, printing, or sharing with your team!

**What you can do:**
- **PDF Export:** Generate professional reports perfect for printing and meetings
- **Excel Export:** Create detailed spreadsheets for analysis and record-keeping

**What's included in exports:**
- Coverage statistics summary
- Complete lists of all oversight assignments
- Contact information for each person
- Coverage gaps (positions needing oversight)
- Organized by role (Overseers, Assistants, Keymen)

**How to use it:**
1. Open the Oversight Dashboard for your event
2. Click **"📄 Export PDF"** or **"📊 Export Excel"** at the top
3. Your report will download automatically

### 📧 Automated Email Notifications

Stay informed with automatic email notifications for important events!

**What you'll receive emails for:**
- **Password Reset:** When an admin resets your password, you'll get an email with your new credentials
- **Account Invitation:** New users receive welcome emails with login instructions
- **Document Publishing:** Get notified when new documents are published for your event
- **Feedback Submissions:** Admins receive alerts when users submit feedback

**What you need to know:**
- Emails are sent automatically - no action needed from you
- All emails use professional TheoShift branding
- Check your spam folder if you don't see an email
