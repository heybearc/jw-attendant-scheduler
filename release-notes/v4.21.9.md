# What's New in Version 4.21.9

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### IVS Approvals — who can manage volunteers

Overseers, assistant overseers, and keymen can now use **Import**, **Export**, **Add volunteer**, and the same editing tools as coordinators when they work on an event—without needing a separate event permission row, in line with how your team already uses those roles elsewhere.

### IVS bulk actions — status, congregation, and department

When you select several IVS volunteers and open **Bulk actions**, you can:

- **Set status** to any of the same choices as the per-person menu: Pending, Requested, Approved, or Not Approved (with an optional shared note when marking Not Approved).
- **Change congregation name** for everyone selected (useful after imports or spelling fixes).
- **Change department name** for everyone selected, in one step.

You can still use bulk actions for **early check-in** and **request round** as before.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- If you use **Not Approved** in bulk without a note, each person keeps their existing denial note where one was already saved.
- **Add volunteer** on the IVS page lets you enter a single IVS volunteer by hand when you do not have a spreadsheet handy.
