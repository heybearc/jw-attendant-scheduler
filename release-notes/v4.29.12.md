# What's New in Version 4.29.12

**Released:** July 29, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### My Profile
Every signed-in user can now open **My Profile** to manage their name, email, phone, congregation, and—where applicable—password.

Profile access is available from:
- Event Selection
- Your name in the event header
- The mobile navigation menu
- The volunteer dashboard and event picker

### Secure account deletion
Users can permanently delete their TheoShift account and personal information.

- Volunteers receive a one-time email confirmation link that expires after 30 minutes
- Staff must re-enter their current password
- Email links open a review page and require a final confirmation
- Admin **View As** sessions cannot edit or delete profiles
- Existing assignments are removed automatically so schedules remain accurate

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Account deletion cannot be undone
- Emailing a volunteer does not delete anything by itself—the user must open the link and confirm
- The final active administrator cannot delete their own account until another administrator is promoted
