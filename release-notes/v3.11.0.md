# What's New in Version 3.11.0

**Released:** February 5, 2026

---

## ✨ UI Modernization - Cleaner, Faster, More Professional

We've completely redesigned the **Volunteers** and **Positions** pages with a modern, professional interface that's easier to use and looks great on all devices!

### What's Better

**Volunteers Page:**
- **Compact Header** - See your volunteer stats at a glance with inline pills showing All/Active/Inactive counts
- **Streamlined Filters** - Quick search and filters in a single horizontal bar (no more scrolling!)
- **Smart Bulk Actions** - Select multiple volunteers and see contextual actions appear automatically
- **Mobile-Friendly** - Works beautifully on phones and tablets

**Positions Page:**
- **Simplified Toolbar** - Reduced from 8+ buttons to just 4 clean primary actions
- **Organized Menus** - Secondary actions (Export, Templates, Notifications) grouped in a "More" menu
- **Professional Icons** - Clean SVG icons replace emoji-heavy buttons
- **Better View Toggle** - Switch between List and Grid views with a sleek segmented control
- **Contextual Actions** - Bulk operations appear only when you need them

### What You'll Notice

**Cleaner Design:**
- Less visual clutter
- More breathing room
- Consistent styling throughout
- Professional appearance similar to modern apps like Airtable and Notion

**Faster Workflow:**
- Everything loads in under 1 second
- Actions are easier to find
- Less clicking to get things done
- Better organization of features

**Mobile Experience:**
- No more horizontal scrolling on phones
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Responsive layout that adapts to your screen

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

**No Action Required!** Everything works exactly the same way - we just made it look better and work smoother.

**All Your Data is Safe** - This is purely a visual update. Your events, volunteers, positions, and assignments are unchanged.

**Same Features, Better Experience** - All the buttons and actions you're used to are still there, just organized more logically.

## 🐛 Known Issues

**Drag-and-Drop Assignment Creation** - We're aware of an issue where dragging volunteers to positions may fail. We're working on a fix for the next update. In the meantime, use the "Assign" button in the position card or the bulk assignment features.
