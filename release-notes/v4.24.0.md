# What's New in Version 4.24.0

**Released:** July 18, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### IVS Early Entry list tools

On **IVS → Approvals**:

- **Sort by Early Entry** — click the Early Entry column header (eligible volunteers rise to the top by default).
- **Filter by Early Entry** — All / Eligible / None, so you can pick a department (for example Accounts) and only see people with early entry days.

### Department on Early Check-In

Early Check-In (staff tab and volunteer desk) now shows each volunteer’s **department**, and search matches name, congregation, or department.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No settings to change — use the new filter and column sort on Approvals, and look for department under the name on check-in.
