# What's New in Version 4.19.0

**Released:** May 2, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Magic link sign-in — email is not case-sensitive

You can type your email in any normal mix of upper and lower case (for example `John@mail.com` or `john@mail.com`). The system still matches you to the correct volunteer record and sends the link as expected.

**What you need to know:** No change to how you use the sign-in page—just less frustration if caps lock or auto-correct change the case.

---

### Desktop volunteer dashboard — documents open in the app

On the **full (desktop) volunteer dashboard**, when you open a published document, it now opens in a **full-screen viewer inside TheoShift** with a clear **Back** control (and you can use the **Escape** key to close). You are no longer sent to a new browser tab where it can be hard to return to the app.

**What you need to know:** Mobile volunteers already had an in-app viewer; desktop behavior now matches that idea.

---

### Progressive Web App (PWA) — service worker enabled again in production

The app can register its service worker on production builds to improve caching of static assets. Full **page navigations** still use the normal browser load so **sign-in and magic links stay reliable**.

**What you need to know:** You may see smoother repeat visits; if anything feels off, contact your administrator.

---

### Feedback — email alerts for every submission path

When someone submits feedback through any supported flow (including forms with attachments), **administrators** continue to receive email notifications when mail is configured. Optional extra notify addresses can be set on the server for coordinators who are not admins (**your administrator can configure this**).

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- **No action required** for most users — sign in and use the volunteer dashboard as before.
- **Feedback and PWA** behavior may be fine-tuned on your server; your team controls email and environment settings.
