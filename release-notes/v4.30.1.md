# What's New in Version 4.30.1

**Released:** July 31, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Request Volunteer now appears for the IVS team
In version 4.30.0 the **Request Volunteer** tab only appeared for people who came in through an IVS import file. In practice that left it hidden for the IVS team members who actually needed it.

Anyone on the roster of an event that uses IVS can now open **Request Volunteer** from the volunteer dashboard — the same people who can use Early Check-In.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Open the volunteer dashboard for an IVS event and choose **Request Volunteer**
- Submissions still arrive as **Pending** on IVS Approvals for staff to review
