# What's New in Version 4.21.4

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Easier bulk selection

On the **Volunteers** list and **IVS Approvals**, click one checkbox, then hold **Shift** and click another row’s checkbox to select everyone **in between** on that page—similar to selecting files on a computer.

### Stay on track when you refresh

Long lists (including Volunteers and IVS) remember how far down the page you had scrolled when you **refresh** the browser, so you are not sent back to the top.

### IVS Approvals: how many rows to show

The **Approvals** tab again includes a **Per page** control so you can choose how many volunteers appear at once (including **All** when you need the full list).

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Shift-select applies to the rows visible on the **current page** of the list.
- No change to how you save or upload data—only how you pick rows and move through long lists.
