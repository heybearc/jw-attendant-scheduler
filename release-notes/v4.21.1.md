# What's New in Version 4.21.1

**Released:** May 6, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Positions page filters

Filters for **Overseer** and **Role** on the event positions page had stopped affecting what you see in list and grid view. They now work the same way across the whole page—including exports—so what you filter is what you get.

If filters hide every position, you will see a short explanation and an easy way to reset overseer and role filters.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required on your side.
- If you use **Assistants** under Role, it looks for assistant overseer accounts linked to volunteers on those assignments.
