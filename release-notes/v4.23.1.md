# What's New in Version 4.23.1

**Released:** June 3, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### IVS Approvals usability

- **Scroll position** — Changing status, early entry days, or other IVS actions no longer jumps you back to the top of the list.
- **Bulk checkboxes** — Row checkboxes now update immediately when you click them (no more waiting for the next row to register the selection).
- **Tab switching** — Moving between Approvals and Early Check-In keeps each tab’s state instead of resetting the page.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required — these are quality-of-life fixes on the IVS module.
