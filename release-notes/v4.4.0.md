## ✨ New Feature: IVS Volunteer Approvals

We're excited to introduce a complete system for managing International Volunteer Service (IVS) volunteers! This new module helps you organize volunteers coming from other locations to serve at your event.

### What you can do:

- **Import volunteer lists** - Upload spreadsheets with volunteer requests from different departments
- **Review and approve** - Easily approve or deny volunteer requests with notes
- **Manage early check-in** - Mark volunteers as eligible for early arrival and check them in ahead of time
- **Bulk operations** - Update multiple volunteers at once (approve, deny, change department, change round)
- **Mobile check-in** - Use a mobile-optimized interface perfect for check-in stations
- **Track everything** - See who submitted each request, which round they're in, and their approval status

### How to use it:

1. **Enable the module:**
   - Go to Admin → Department Templates
   - Edit your template and go to the Modules tab
   - Turn on "IVS Approvals"
   - Save

2. **Access IVS Approvals:**
   - Navigate to your event
   - Click the "IVS Approvals" tab
   - You'll see options to import, export, and manage volunteers

3. **Import volunteers:**
   - Click "Import Volunteers"
   - Upload your spreadsheet (Excel or CSV)
   - The system validates and imports your list

4. **Manage approvals:**
   - Review each volunteer request
   - Click "Approve" or "Deny" with a reason
   - Or use bulk actions to process multiple requests at once

5. **Mobile check-in:**
   - Click "📱 Mobile Check-In" for a simplified interface
   - Search for volunteers by name or congregation
   - Tap the big green "Check In" button
   - Perfect for volunteers working check-in tables on tablets or phones!

## 🎯 What Makes This Special

**Separate from Local Volunteers**
- IVS volunteers are managed separately from your local volunteers
- They only appear on the IVS Approvals page, keeping everything organized

**Built for Mobile**
- The mobile check-in interface is optimized for touch devices
- Large buttons, clear search, and real-time stats
- Works great on phones and tablets

**Powerful Bulk Operations**
- Select multiple volunteers and update them all at once
- Change departments, rounds, or approval status in seconds
- Save time when processing large lists

**Complete Tracking**
- See who submitted each request and when
- Track which round each volunteer is in
- Monitor early check-in eligibility and actual check-in times
- Export everything to spreadsheets for reporting

## 💡 What You Need to Know

**No action required!** The IVS Approvals module is optional and only appears when you enable it in your department template.

**For administrators:**
- Enable IVS Approvals in your department template to start using it
- Check out the new Help Center page: Help → IVS Volunteer Approvals
- The mobile check-in URL can be shared with volunteers working check-in stations

**IVS volunteers are separate:**
- They won't appear on your main Volunteers page
- This keeps your local volunteers and IVS volunteers organized separately
- You can still export and manage them just like regular volunteers
