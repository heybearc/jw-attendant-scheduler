# What's New in Version 4.22.0

**Released:** June 3, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Clearer confirmations and messages across the app

TheoShift no longer uses the browser’s generic popup boxes for everyday actions. Instead you get:

- **Toast notifications** at the bottom of the screen for success, errors, and tips (they dismiss on their own).
- **Confirmation dialogs** in the app when you delete something, send bulk emails, clear assignments, undo check-ins, and similar actions.
- **Typed confirmations** where safety matters (for example, typing **DELETE** before removing an event).

This matches the experience on our other Cloudigan apps and works better on phones.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No settings to change — the new dialogs appear automatically.
- **Cancel** on a confirmation simply closes the dialog; nothing is sent or deleted.
