# What's New in Version 4.29.13

**Released:** July 30, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Document publish uses the Volunteers roster only
When you publish a document to **All Volunteers** or pick individuals, TheoShift now includes only people on the event **Volunteers roster**.

IVS Module people are not notified or given the document unless they were also added to the Volunteers roster.

**What you will see:**
- Publish modal shows **All Volunteers on roster (N people)** with the roster count
- Individual selection lists the same roster people
- A short note under the options explains the roster-only rule

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- If someone needs a published document and only appears under IVS, add them to the Volunteers roster first (they can stay on IVS as well)
