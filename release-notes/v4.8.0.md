# What's New in Version 4.8.0

**Released:** February 15, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### IVS Module with Tab Navigation

We've completely redesigned how you manage IVS volunteers! The IVS Module now has a clean tab-based interface that makes it easier to switch between managing approvals and checking in volunteers.

**What you can do:**
- Access both Approvals and Early Check-In from one central location
- Switch between tabs without navigating to different pages
- Enjoy a more streamlined workflow

**How to use it:**
1. Navigate to your event
2. Click the **IVS Module** tab
3. Use the **Approvals** tab to manage volunteer requests
4. Use the **⏰ Early Check-In** tab to check in volunteers on event day

---

### Improved Early Check-In Experience

The Early Check-In interface has been redesigned with a single-page layout featuring collapsible sections for better organization.

**What you can do:**
- See pending and checked-in volunteers on the same page
- Collapse sections to focus on what matters
- View real-time stats (pending, checked in, total eligible)
- Automatic updates every 5 seconds for multi-user coordination

**What you'll see:**
- **PENDING CHECK-IN** section (orange) - Volunteers waiting to check in
- **CHECKED IN** section (green) - Volunteers who have already checked in
- Live status indicator showing last update time
- Large, touch-friendly check-in buttons for mobile devices

---

### Volunteer Portal Early Check-In

IVS volunteers can now check themselves in using a dedicated volunteer portal page.

**What you can do:**
- Share a direct link with volunteers for self-service check-in
- Reduce check-in table congestion
- Empower volunteers to manage their own check-in

**How it works:**
1. Volunteers visit the early check-in portal
2. They enter their name and congregation
3. System verifies their eligibility
4. They check themselves in with one tap

---

## 🔒 Security Improvements

### Early Entry Flag Protection

We've added validation to prevent accidentally removing the early entry flag from volunteers who are already checked in.

**What was fixed:**
- System now prevents unchecking "Early Entry Eligible" if volunteer is already checked in
- Helps maintain data integrity and prevents confusion
- Clear error messages guide you if you try to make this change

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

### Module Name Change

The "IVS Approvals" module is now called "IVS Module" to better reflect its expanded functionality with both approvals and check-in features.

**What this means for you:**
- Look for "IVS Module" in your event navigation
- Department template settings now show "IVS Module" instead of "IVS Approvals"
- All functionality remains the same, just with a clearer name

### No Action Required

All changes are automatically available. Your existing IVS volunteer data and settings are preserved.
