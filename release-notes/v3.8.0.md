# What's New in Version 3.8.0

**Released:** February 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### 📱 Mobile Volunteer Dashboard

We've completely redesigned the volunteer experience for mobile devices! Access your assignments, respond to availability requests, and view documents—all from your phone.

**What you can do:**
- View all your position assignments in one place
- Respond to availability requests on the go
- Contact overseers with one tap (call or email)
- View and download published event documents
- Sign out easily when needed

**How to use it:**
1. Open your mobile browser (Safari or Chrome)
2. Go to the volunteer login page
3. Enter your name, congregation, and PIN
4. Access your personalized dashboard with 4 tabs:
   - **📋 Assignments** - See all your positions
   - **📅 Availability** - Respond to requests
   - **👥 Contacts** - Quick access to overseers
   - **📄 Documents** - View published documents

**New in this version:**
- Touch-friendly buttons (easy to tap on small screens)
- Fast loading times (optimized for mobile networks)
- Beautiful, modern design
- Works on iPhone and Android

---

### 📄 Document Management for Volunteers

Volunteers can now view all documents published to them directly from their mobile dashboard.

**What you'll see:**
- Document title and description
- File size and type (PDF, images, videos)
- When the document was published
- One-tap button to view or download

**How to access:**
1. Login to your volunteer dashboard
2. Tap the **Documents** tab
3. Tap "View Document" to open any document

---

### 🚪 Easy Sign Out

Need to logout? We've added a sign out button to the mobile dashboard.

**How to use it:**
1. Look for the logout icon in the top-right corner
2. Tap the icon to sign out
3. You'll be returned to the login page
