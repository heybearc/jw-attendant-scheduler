# What's New in Version 4.15.0

**Released:** February 19, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Global Announcements Banner

Administrators can now broadcast important messages to all users directly within the app. When an active announcement is published, a color-coded banner appears at the top of every page — so no one misses critical updates.

**What you can do:**
- See important announcements the moment you log in
- Dismiss banners you've already read
- View announcements color-coded by type: Info, Warning, or Alert

**How it works:**
1. An administrator creates an announcement in the **Admin → Announcements** section
2. The banner automatically appears for all logged-in users
3. Click the ✕ to dismiss a banner for your current session

---

### Announcements Tab in Admin Navigation

The admin navigation bar now includes a dedicated **Announcements** tab, making it easy to manage global announcements without hunting through menus.

**What you can do:**
- Quickly access the announcements management page from any admin screen
- Create, edit, and deactivate announcements in one place

---

### Improved Offline Support (PWA)

The TheoShift app now works better when your internet connection is slow or unavailable. Volunteer information and pages load faster and remain accessible even in low-connectivity environments.

**What you'll notice:**
- Volunteer dashboard and check-in pages load more reliably on spotty connections
- The app remembers recently viewed volunteer data so it's available offline
- Pending check-ins are saved and automatically submitted when connectivity returns

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required — the announcements banner and improved offline support are active automatically
- Administrators: use **Admin → Announcements** to create your first global announcement
- Volunteers: the app will continue to work normally; offline improvements happen in the background
