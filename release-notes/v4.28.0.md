# What's New in Version 4.28.0

**Released:** July 25, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Phone numbers formatted everywhere

Phone fields across TheoShift now use **(XXX) XXX-XXXX** when you type or paste (including `+1`). That includes volunteers, attendants, admin users, event overseer/assistants/keymen, department contacts, volunteer profile, and CSV import.

Existing phone numbers in the database were normalized to the same format. Lists, popups, and assignment emails also display phones consistently.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required — open any phone field and it will format as you edit.
- IVS department contacts stay where they are: manage on Approvals; look up on Early Check-In (including the volunteer Early Check-In tab for IVS team).
