# What's New in Version 4.21.6

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Email to selected volunteers

Sending an email to **people you checked off** on the Volunteers page now reaches the correct roster members every time, including cases where the list was built in different ways behind the scenes.

If something still blocks delivery (for example, email settings on the server), you’ll get a **clearer message** instead of a confusing failure.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- **Email all active volunteers** works as before.
- If you see a prompt to refresh and try again, reload the Volunteers page so the list matches the server, then resend.
