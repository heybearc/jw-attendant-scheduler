# What's New in Version 4.21.3

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## ✨ Improvements

### Easier navigation on your phone or tablet

When you open an event on a smaller screen, you no longer need to swipe a long row of tabs sideways. You will see which **section** you are in (for example, Overview or Volunteers) and a **More** button that lists every section so you can jump in one tap.

The same idea applies inside **Event Settings**, so settings sub-sections are easier to reach without awkward horizontal scrolling.

### Volunteers and Positions on mobile

The **Volunteers** and **Positions** pages are tuned for touch: clearer buttons, card-style layouts where it helps, and filters that are easier to use with your thumb.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required. Use **More** wherever you used to slide tabs on a phone or tablet.
