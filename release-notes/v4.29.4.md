# What's New in Version 4.29.4

**Released:** July 27, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Email blasts no longer include IVS-only people
Notify Chat Launch and other bulk emails were sending to everyone linked to the event, including people who only appear on the IVS Module.

**What changed:**
- Bulk emails go to the Volunteers roster only
- You see how many people will be emailed and can cancel before sending
- Large sends are paced for Gmail safety, and you can Abort a send in progress
- Coordinator messages in availability emails keep paragraph spacing
- Volunteers can leave a comment when responding to availability (required for Partial), and it is saved to their profile notes

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required
- Prefer Abort over restarting the server if a bulk email is still running
