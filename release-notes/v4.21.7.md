# What's New in Version 4.21.7

**Released:** May 8, 2026

---

## 🐛 Reliability

### Email many volunteers without timeouts

When you send an email to **many volunteers at once** from the event Volunteers page, the system now completes the job reliably instead of stopping early because of a server timeout.

You may see progress while messages are sent in the background.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No change to **who** receives mail—only how large sends are processed.
- If email is not configured in TheoShift, you will still see the same clear message as before.
