# What's New in Version 4.25.0

**Released:** July 20, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### IVS department contacts

You can now save **overseer and assistant contacts by IVS department** (for example Accounts, Safety).

**On Approvals:** open **Department contacts**, pick or add a department, and enter overseer name / phone / email plus assistants.

**On Early Check-In:** use the same panel in lookup mode when someone says they are with a department but is not on the early-entry list — tap phone or email to reach the right people right away.

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- Contacts are stored per event — set them once for the convention.
- Department names should match the department labels used on IVS volunteer rows.
