# What's New in Version 4.16.0

**Released:** April 19, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### 📧 Magic Link Authentication (Passwordless Login)

Volunteers can now sign in without remembering a password! Just enter your email address, and we'll send you a secure sign-in link.

**What you can do:**
- Sign in from any device using just your email
- No password to remember or reset
- More secure than traditional passwords
- Works great on mobile devices

**How to use it:**
1. Go to the login page
2. Select "Volunteer" at the top
3. Choose "Email Link" method
4. Enter your email address
5. Click "Send Sign-In Link"
6. Check your email and click the link
7. You're signed in automatically!

**Security:** The link expires in 24 hours and can only be used once.

---

### 🎯 Unified Login Page

We've redesigned the login experience to make it easier and more intuitive.

**What's new:**
- One login page for everyone (volunteers and oversight)
- Easy toggle between "Oversight" and "Volunteer" roles
- Clear visual indicators showing which role you're signing in as
- Your role selection is remembered for next time

**How it works:**
- Click "Oversight" or "Volunteer" at the top of the login page
- The form changes to show the right login options for your role
- Your choice is saved automatically

---

### 🔢 PIN Login Still Available

Don't worry - if you prefer using your PIN, it still works exactly the same way!

**What you can do:**
- Continue using your 4-digit PIN for quick access
- No changes to the PIN login process
- Choose between Email Link or PIN based on your preference

**How to use it:**
1. Go to the login page
2. Select "Volunteer" at the top
3. Choose "PIN Login" method
4. Enter your name, congregation, and PIN
5. Click "Sign In"

---

## 💡 What You Need to Know

### For Volunteers

**No action required!** You can continue using your PIN login as before, or try the new Email Link method for easier access.

**Which method should you use?**
- **Email Link:** More secure, no password to remember, works from any device
- **PIN Login:** Faster if you remember your PIN

**Tip:** We recommend trying the Email Link method - it's easier and more secure!

### For Administrators

**No action required!** The new login system works automatically. Volunteers can choose their preferred login method.

**What to know:**
- All existing volunteer accounts work with both methods
- Email links are sent using your existing email configuration
- Old volunteer login URLs automatically redirect to the new page
- PIN login remains fully functional for backward compatibility
