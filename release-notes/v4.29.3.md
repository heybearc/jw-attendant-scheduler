# What's New in Version 4.29.3

**Released:** July 26, 2026

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Someone on IVS couldn't be added as a volunteer
If a person was already on the **IVS Module** for the event, adding them on the **Volunteers** page said they were already on the event — even though they did not appear on Volunteers or Positions.

**What changed:**
- Adding them with the same email now places them on the volunteer roster
- They stay on IVS and also appear on Volunteers/Positions for assignments
- If they are already on the roster, you get a clear info message instead of an error

**What you need to do:**
- No action required — add them again from Volunteers if you still need them on the roster
