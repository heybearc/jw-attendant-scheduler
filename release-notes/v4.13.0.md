# What's New in Version 4.13.0

**Released:** February 18, 2026

---

## ✨ New Features

### Conflict Detection When Assigning Volunteers

When you assign a volunteer to a shift, TheoShift now automatically checks whether that volunteer is already assigned to another overlapping shift — before you submit.

**What you'll see:**
- Volunteers with no conflicts appear at the top of the list
- An amber **Conflict** badge appears next to any volunteer with a scheduling conflict
- Selecting a conflicted volunteer shows a message explaining exactly which position and shift overlaps
- Conflict-free volunteers are always sorted to the top for easier selection

**Types of conflicts detected:**
- Time overlap with another shift
- All-day shift conflicts
- Duplicate shift assignment

**You're still in control** — coordinators can override any conflict warning and assign the volunteer anyway. The warnings are there to help, not to block.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

### Assignment Email Now Shows Correct Shift Details

Assignment notification emails now correctly display:
- The shift name (e.g. "Morning 1")
- **All Day** for all-day shifts instead of "Not specified"
- Combined time display (e.g. "9:00 AM – 11:00 AM") instead of separate start/end rows

### Email Wording Improvements

- All emails now say "contact your overseer" instead of "contact your event coordinator"
- Subject lines are cleaner and easier to read at a glance
- Removed generic preparation checklist from reminder emails

## 💡 What You Need to Know

- No action required — conflict detection works automatically when you open the Assign Volunteer dialog
- Existing assignments are not affected
- Coordinators can still override conflict warnings if needed
